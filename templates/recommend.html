Soil Analysis

N-P-K values are converted into a Soil Health Score (0-100). pH near 6.5 is ideal for most crops.

Crop Rotation Logic

  • After a cereal (e.g. Rice) → prefer pulses / oilseeds.
  • After a legume/pulse → a cereal is a good next choice.
  • Same crop family consecutively increases disease carryover risk.
  • Legumes restore soil nitrogen between heavy feeders.

Output

Top crop + 3 alternatives with Suitability %, Rotation Score, Soil Compatibility, Water Requirement, Season Suitability and an explanation for each choice.
